Remnescens

Remnescens is an independent studio dedicated to creating immersive psychological experiences. Our mission is to explore the boundaries of interactive narrative, provoking reflections on memory, identity, and silence.

Inspired by historical events, cognitive theories, and sensory atmospheres, the studio seeks to create worlds that respond emotionally to the player's choices.

We utilize dynamic response systems and reactive design so that every journey is unique — not just played, but felt.

The Creator

Luiz is the mind behind the Remnescens universe. A solo developer, writer, and interactive narrative researcher, he builds worlds where the player doesn't just observe but participates in the reconstruction of their own psyche.

With a hybrid background in technology and humanities, Luiz believes that games can be vehicles for profound introspection — and that silence, when properly explored, speaks louder than any dialogue.
